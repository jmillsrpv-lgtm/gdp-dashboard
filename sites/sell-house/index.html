Common questions

How do you come up with your offer?

We look at what similar homes nearby have sold for after being fixed up, subtract the cost of repairs, and factor in our holding and selling costs. We'll walk you through the numbers so you can see exactly how we got there.

Is there any cost or obligation?

No. Getting an offer is free and you're never obligated to accept it.

Do I have to clean out the house?

No. Take what you want and leave the rest — we'll handle it.

What if the house is still in probate?

We work with inherited properties often. Depending on where the estate is in the process, we can usually agree on terms now and close once the executor or administrator has authority to sell. Talk to your estate attorney about your specific situation.

Are you real estate agents?

No. We're a local real estate investment company that buys houses directly. We may buy the property ourselves or with an investment partner, and we'll always tell you who the buyer is.

How fast can you close?

Typically 14–21 days once we have a clear title, but we can close later if you need more time to move.
